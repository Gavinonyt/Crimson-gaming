Connect Discord Rich Presence

Show your friends when you're using Ultra Launcher and which Ultra game you're playing.

1. Create your Discord applicationOpen the Discord Developer Portal, choose New Application, and give it a name such as Ultra Launcher.
2. Copy its Application IDIn your application, open General Information and copy the Application ID. Do not paste a bot token or secret here.
Keep the Discord desktop app running. This companion uses Discord's local Rich Presence connection.

Your Application ID is stored locally on this PC. Ultra Launcher does not need your Discord password, bot token, or account secret.
