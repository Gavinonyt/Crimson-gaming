# Crimson Gaming 2.5.0
**Release date:** November 19, 2026

## 2.5.0 — Crimson Originals & Cockpit Update

### Crimson Originals beta arcade
- Added playable browser beta builds for Crimson Drift, Crimson Life, Crimson Quest, Crimson Blocks, and Crimson Racing.
- Added Crimson Launcher as an in-site hub for launching the Crimson Originals.
- Added a dedicated Arcade page and Store launch actions for each title.
- Replaced the previous Coming Soon and In Development labels with beta-release labels.
- These are first playable browser builds, not full-scale downloadable desktop games.

### Crimson Sprint vehicle interiors
- Improved the in-game cockpit view with distinct styling for vehicle families, including sports cars, muscle cars, trucks, SUVs, and electric vehicles.
- Added or refined dashboard shapes, instrument displays, infotainment screens, climate controls, steering-wheel details, seat silhouettes, trim, and stitching.
- Interior designs are stylized, vehicle-inspired game renderings. They are not exact, factory-verified replicas of every real vehicle.

## Previous updates

### 2.4.1 — Real American Brands
- Refined the dealership lineup to focus on recognizable real American automakers and established specialty brands.
- Kept core names including Chevrolet, Ford, Cadillac, GMC, Lincoln, Dodge, Ram, Jeep, Chrysler, Buick, Tesla, Rivian, and Lucid, alongside selected classic American makes and truck manufacturers.

### 2.4.0 — American Legends Update
- Expanded the American vehicle collection with additional marques and models.
- The dealership lineup was subsequently refined in 2.4.1.

### 2.2.0 — City & Themes Update
- Expanded the original Crimson City map with a Miami-inspired coastal layout.
- Added Vice Sunset, Ocean Drive, Emerald Night, and Midnight Gold themes.
